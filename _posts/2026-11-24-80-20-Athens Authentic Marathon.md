At 8th of November 2026 I run Athens Authentic Marathon and the result
provides the ultimate verdict on 80-20 method: it works for me.

I planned 4:30 and did it in 4:36:14, without excessively tiring myself, even if the course is
360m ups and downs. I did the fast finish, last 2 km, meaning I was not spent.
It was ideal weather, not full sun but mixed with clouds, and not too warm,
just pleasant 15 cels.

After Poznań last month I followed, prompted by experienced runner, the last month of
preparations once again. This time very carefully, so about 100%, and I was lucky enough
not to fall on flu or something meantime. Also injuries-I was afraid of overdoing, as I was
well into more than 18 weeks of training...but no, thanks Matt Fitzgerald's advice in the book,
I could fully enjoy what I trained for.

Next steps? Improve to just below 4h in a year, for Ljubljana in October 2027?
Maybe, let's see, no need for pressure, I just want to have a motivation to
continue and improve. On the way to this, as part of the training, is sub-20 5K
and sub 2hrs half-marathon.
